frooms

Experiment: how often do bookings change during the day?

frooms fetches every room once a night for the coming week, but during the day it only refreshes seminar and meeting rooms. To see what that misses, each prefetch run also re-checks two of the other rooms (lecture halls, labs, computer rooms, …) and compares today's and tomorrow's bookings with what was stored. The regular refresh of seminar and meeting rooms is compared the same way, as a baseline.

A change is upcoming if the booking hadn't ended when it was noticed: until then frooms would have shown the room as free while it was booked (new booking) or busy while it was free (dropped booking).
