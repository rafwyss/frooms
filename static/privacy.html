Privacy notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026

frooms shows free rooms, study spaces and menus at ETH Zurich and the University of Zurich. With an account you can also add friends, let them see where you are studying, rate dishes and other people's occupancy reports, and collect street cred (points for what you do on frooms). This notice explains what personal data frooms processes, why, where it is stored, who sees it and what rights you have. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Who is responsible

frooms is run by Raffaele Wyss, a private person in Zurich, Switzerland.
Contact for all privacy questions: info@frooms.ch

frooms is an independent project. It is not run by ETH Zurich or the University of Zurich, and neither of them receives data about you from frooms.

Using frooms without an account

You can use everything except friends, check-ins, Street cred and rating dishes or reports without an account. In that case:

  • Requests to our server. Like every website, our server receives your IP address and technical details about your browser with each request. We use the IP address only briefly to limit excessive requests and don't store it ourselves. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, keeps technical logs for a few days for operation and security.
  • Visitor statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't create profiles of individual visitors.
  • Usage counts. frooms counts how often parts of the page are used (for example searches, tabs opened, menus opened) and some account actions (sign-ins, check-ins, ratings), as totals per day. The counts carry no identifier of you, your browser or your account, never what you searched for, and can't be linked to a person. We keep them to see how frooms is used.
  • Settings on your device. Your choices (the tab, area, buildings and filters you picked, and places you starred) are stored in your browser (local storage). Without an account they never leave your device; with one, your starred places are also kept with your account (see below). You can delete them by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Your location on the map. If you allow it, frooms uses your device's location to show nearby places. It is worked out on your device and is never sent to us, with or without an account.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback, we store your message, the category, the time and some context (the tab, area, buildings and filters you had open, the room you had open and your screen size), so we can understand and fix the issue. Your email address is stored only if you give one, and only so we can reply. Each feedback is also forwarded to our mailbox (info@frooms.ch, hosted by Infomaniak in Switzerland). If you report a person (from your friends list), your report names their username. Feedback is never linked to your account, also when you send it signed in (see "Street cred" for the points it earns), with one exception: a bug report sent signed in with "Report a bug". It also describes the element you tapped (its name on the page and its visible text, no screenshot), and it is linked to your account until we have decided on its reward, so the points can reach you and you can see the outcome.
  • Occupancy reports. If you report how full a place is, frooms stores the report with the time and a random id of your browser (so one browser counts once); never your location or IP address. We store the id only scrambled (as a SHA-256 hash), so it can't be read back. Your browser also keeps the id and your reports of the past 2 hours, so the page can show what you reported. Everyone sees how many reports a place got in the past 2 hours, and their times and levels for the past 4 hours, never who sent them. A rating of frooms' estimate is stored the same way and never shown. We keep the reports to learn how full places get. If you report while signed in, frooms also notes for a short time that the report is yours (see "Street cred, ratings and votes").

Your account

An account is voluntary. You need one to add friends, to check in, to rate dishes and reports and to collect street cred. Accounts are open to members of ETH Zurich and the University of Zurich (students and staff).

Signing in with SWITCH edu-ID

You sign in with your SWITCH edu-ID, the same login you use for your university. You enter your password on SWITCH's page, never on frooms; frooms never sees it. After you sign in, SWITCH tells frooms:

  • an identifier for you that is used only for frooms (other services get a different one);
  • your first and last name;
  • your email address;
  • your affiliation with a university (for example "student at ETH Zurich").

SWITCH and your university process your edu-ID under their own privacy policies (see Switch edu-ID's privacy policy). SWITCH records that you signed in to frooms.

What we store

  • the identifier from edu-ID;
  • your first and last name, refreshed from edu-ID each time you sign in;
  • your university (ETH or UZH), taken from the affiliation. We don't store any further details of it;
  • your email address only in scrambled form (as a SHA-256 hash), so that someone who types your exact address can find you. We can't read your address from it, and we never send you emails;
  • the username and the look you choose for your bubble (the circle with your initials): two colours, and once you have unlocked them, a second colour for a gradient or a metal (bronze, silver or gold);
  • your starred places, so they are the same on every device where you sign in. Nobody else sees them;
  • when you created the account and last used it, and a random key for each browser where you are signed in (stored only scrambled, without any details of the browser or device).

There are no passwords and no profile pictures.

Why: to sign you in, to make sure accounts belong to members of the two universities, to let friends find and recognise you, and to keep accounts secure.

Cookies

frooms sets two cookies, and only when you sign in: one for the sign-in itself, which lasts 10 minutes, and one that keeps you signed in, which ends 30 days after you last used frooms. Both are needed for signing in. They are not used for advertising or tracking. Without an account frooms sets no cookies.

What other users see of you

  • Anyone who is signed in can find you by your username or your name, or by typing your exact email address. They see your username, your first name with the first letter of your last name ("Raffaele W.") and your bubble. Your email address is never shown to anyone.
  • If you join the leaderboards, others who joined them also see your street cred there (see below).
  • Nothing else is visible to people who aren't your friends. Your street cred log, your meal ratings, your votes and which occupancy reports are yours are never shown to anyone else.

Friends

  • You become friends only when one of you sends a request and the other accepts it.
  • We store who your friends are, the requests you sent or received and haven't answered, and the people you blocked.
  • Either of you can end the friendship at any time. The other person isn't told.
  • If you block someone, you no longer find each other and they can't send you requests.

Check-ins

A check-in tells your friends where you are studying. frooms never checks you in by itself and never follows your location.

  • When: only when you pick a place and check in.
  • What we store: the place you picked (a room, a study space or library, a mensa or cafeteria; never just a building), the time you checked in, the end time, the time you checked out or the check-in ended, how it ended (by you, by the end time or by a new check-in) and how you started it (from the places near you, from the search or from a place's page).
  • What we don't store: your position. Your device's location is used only on your device, to suggest the places around you. We also don't verify that you are where you say.
  • Who sees it: your friends, and only while the check-in runs. They see the place, since when and until when. Nobody else sees it, and it isn't public.
  • How it ends: when you check out, at the end time, or after 3 hours if you set no time. From then on your friends no longer see it.
  • Invites: if you send an invite link, anyone who has the link sees the place and time in it. You decide who gets it.

Street cred, ratings and votes

Signed in, you earn street cred (points) for things other people can see or use, and you can rate dishes and other people's occupancy reports. Without an account nothing of this is stored, and nothing is remembered for later.

  • Your points log: each time you earn points we store what earned them (a new friendship, a check-in, an occupancy report, a 👍 on one of your reports, a vote on someone's report, an invite you sent, a problem or feedback you sent, a dish you rated), the points and the time. For check-ins, reports and dishes the entry also has the place's or dish's name; for a friendship, who it was with. Your total, your level and the number of 👍 your reports received are kept with your account. Only you see your log.
  • Why the friendship entry stays: points for a friendship come once per pair, so the entry that you two were friends stays after the friendship ends, as long as both accounts exist. It isn't shown to anyone but you.
  • Occupancy reports and votes: when you report while signed in, we note which account sent the report, so that 👍 from others reach you and you can't vote on your own report. When you vote 👍 or 👎 on a report, we store your vote. Both are deleted after 2 days. Others see only how many 👍 and 👎 a report got, never who sent it or who voted. If your reports have received 20 👍 or more in total, they are marked "trusted reporter" in the report log, without saying who you are.
  • Meal ratings: we store your rating (taste, portion size, value for money) with the dish, the day, the mensa and the time. Others see only each dish's averages and how many people rated it; you see your own values.
  • Leaderboards: only if you join them. Then others who also joined see your username, your name as "Raffaele W.", your bubble, your rank and your points. People who haven't joined don't see the leaderboards at all. You can leave at any time, and you are gone from them at once.
  • Your bubble's look (colours, gradient or metal) is seen wherever your bubble is shown: by people who find you, your friends, and on the leaderboards if you joined them.
  • Feedback: sending a problem report or feedback while signed in earns points, but the feedback itself is stored without any link to your account.
  • Bug reports: a bug report sent signed in with "Report a bug" earns 50 points once we have checked it. Until then, and for 90 days after we decide, your account keeps a link to it: the start of your message, its state (pending, rewarded or not rewarded) and, if not rewarded, our short reason. Only you and frooms' admins see it.

What we learn from how frooms is used

We keep ended check-ins to understand which rooms and study spaces are used, when, and for how long, and to improve frooms with that (for example by showing how busy a place usually is).

  • For this we only add up check-ins across all users. We don't evaluate what an individual person does, and we don't create profiles.
  • Points are counted per account only to show you your own street cred and, if you join them, the leaderboards. Nothing else is derived from them, and they aren't used to judge or rank anyone otherwise.
  • Statistics we show or publish never identify a person.
  • After 12 months, and at once when you delete your account, we remove the link between a check-in and you. What remains is a place and times, without any reference to a person.
  • We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't pass it on to anyone, including the universities.

We don't record what you search for, whose profile you look at, or which pages you open while signed in. Your friends' check-ins are fetched every 2 minutes while frooms is open; that isn't recorded either.

How long we keep data

  • Account data, starred places, friends, requests and blocks: as long as your account exists. An account that hasn't been used for 24 months is deleted, as if you had deleted it yourself.
  • Signed-in browsers: until you sign out, or 30 days after you last used frooms in them. Signing out of frooms doesn't sign you out of edu-ID.
  • A running check-in: visible to friends until it ends.
  • Ended check-ins: linked to you for 12 months, then kept without any link to a person.
  • Street cred: each entry of your points log for 12 months (a friendship's entry as long as both accounts exist). Your total, level, number of 👍 received and whether you joined the leaderboards: as long as your account exists.
  • Which reports are yours, and your votes on reports: 2 days.
  • Meal ratings: 60 days after the day the dish was served, as long as the menus.
  • When you delete your account: your account data, the starred places kept with it, friends, requests, blocks, signed-in browsers, your points log, meal ratings, votes, the links to your reports and your bug reports' links are deleted at once, a running check-in ends, and your check-ins lose their link to you. Others keep the points they earned through you (a friendship, a 👍), without any reference to you; your votes no longer count. The starred places in your browser stay until you remove them. There is nothing to delete at SWITCH: your edu-ID stays as it is.
  • Feedback: as long as it is useful for improving frooms, at most 2 years.
  • A bug report's link to your account: until we decide on its reward, then 90 days (at most 12 months in all).
  • Occupancy reports: as usage data, without an end date. They contain no IP address and no readable id.
  • Usage counts (totals per day, without any identifier): without an end date.
  • Server logs at Cloudflare: a few days.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time:

  • what personal data we hold about you (right of access),
  • to correct wrong data,
  • to delete your data, or to stop processing it,
  • to receive the data you gave us in a common electronic format.

You can change your username and your bubble yourself in your profile, join or leave the leaderboards in Street cred, and delete your account yourself under Account. Your name comes from edu-ID; to change it, change it there. For everything else write to info@frooms.ch. We reply within 30 days and free of charge. To protect your data, we may ask you to confirm that you own the account.

If you think we are not handling your data correctly, you can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), www.edoeb.admin.ch.

Security

Connections to frooms are always encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the databases and service accounts is limited to the operator and protected by strong authentication. If a breach of data security is likely to put you at high risk, we will tell you and the FDPIC.

Changes

If we change how frooms handles personal data, we update this notice and the date at the top. For important changes that affect your account, we will tell you in the app before they apply.

Dein Konto

Ein Konto ist freiwillig. Du brauchst es, um Freundinnen und Freunde hinzuzufügen, um einzuchecken, um Gerichte und Meldungen zu bewerten und um Street Cred zu sammeln. Ein Konto können Angehörige der ETH Zürich und der Universität Zürich (Studierende und Mitarbeitende) eröffnen.

Anmeldung mit SWITCH edu-ID

Check-ins

Mit einem Check-in zeigst du deinen Freundinnen und Freunden, wo du lernst. frooms checkt dich nie von selbst ein und verfolgt deinen Standort nie.

  • Wann: nur wenn du einen Ort auswählst und eincheckst.
  • Was wir speichern: den Ort, den du gewählt hast (einen Raum, einen Lernplatz oder eine Bibliothek, eine Mensa oder Cafeteria; nie nur ein Gebäude), den Zeitpunkt des Check-ins, die Endzeit, den Zeitpunkt, an dem du ausgecheckt hast oder der Check-in geendet hat, wie er geendet hat (durch dich, durch die Endzeit oder durch einen neuen Check-in) und wie du ihn gestartet hast (über die Orte in deiner Nähe, über die Suche oder über die Seite eines Orts).
  • Was wir nicht speichern: deine Position. Der Standort deines Geräts wird nur auf deinem Gerät verwendet, um dir die Orte um dich herum vorzuschlagen. Wir prüfen auch nicht, ob du wirklich dort bist.
  • Wer ihn sieht: deine Freundinnen und Freunde, und nur solange der Check-in läuft. Sie sehen den Ort, seit wann und bis wann. Sonst sieht ihn niemand, und er ist nicht öffentlich.
  • Wie er endet: wenn du auscheckst, zur Endzeit oder nach 3 Stunden, wenn du keine Zeit gesetzt hast. Danach sehen ihn deine Freundinnen und Freunde nicht mehr.
  • Einladungen: Wenn du einen Einladungslink verschickst, sieht jede Person mit dem Link den Ort und die Zeit darin. Du entscheidest, wer ihn bekommt.

Street Cred, Bewertungen und Stimmen

Angemeldet erhältst du Street Cred (Punkte) für das, was andere sehen oder nutzen können, und du kannst Gerichte und die Belegungsmeldungen anderer bewerten. Ohne Konto wird davon nichts gespeichert und nichts für später vorgemerkt.

  • Dein Punkteverlauf: Jedes Mal, wenn du Punkte erhältst, speichern wir, wofür (eine neue Freundschaft, ein Check-in, eine Belegungsmeldung, ein 👍 auf eine deiner Meldungen, eine Stimme zur Meldung einer anderen Person, eine verschickte Einladung, ein gemeldetes Problem oder Feedback, ein bewertetes Gericht), die Punkte und den Zeitpunkt. Bei Check-ins, Meldungen und Gerichten enthält der Eintrag zudem den Namen des Orts oder Gerichts, bei einer Freundschaft, mit wem. Deine Gesamtpunktzahl, dein Level und die Zahl der 👍 auf deine Meldungen werden bei deinem Konto gespeichert. Nur du siehst deinen Verlauf.
  • Warum der Eintrag zur Freundschaft bleibt: Punkte für eine Freundschaft gibt es pro Paar nur einmal. Deshalb bleibt der Eintrag, dass ihr befreundet wart, auch nach dem Ende der Freundschaft bestehen, solange beide Konten bestehen. Er wird niemandem ausser dir angezeigt.
  • Belegungsmeldungen und Stimmen: Wenn du angemeldet meldest, halten wir fest, von welchem Konto die Meldung stammt, damit 👍 anderer bei dir ankommen und du nicht über deine eigene Meldung abstimmen kannst. Wenn du eine Meldung mit 👍 oder 👎 bewertest, speichern wir deine Stimme. Beides wird nach 2 Tagen gelöscht. Andere sehen nur, wie viele 👍 und 👎 eine Meldung erhalten hat, nie, wer sie gesendet oder abgestimmt hat. Haben deine Meldungen insgesamt 20 👍 oder mehr erhalten, werden sie im Meldungsverlauf als «vertrauenswürdige Quelle» markiert, ohne zu zeigen, wer du bist.
  • Bewertungen von Gerichten: Wir speichern deine Bewertung (Geschmack, Portionengrösse, Preis-Leistung) mit dem Gericht, dem Tag, der Mensa und dem Zeitpunkt. Andere sehen nur die Durchschnitte jedes Gerichts und wie viele es bewertet haben; du siehst deine eigenen Werte.
  • Ranglisten: nur, wenn du mitmachst. Dann sehen andere, die ebenfalls mitmachen, deinen Benutzernamen, deinen Namen als «Raffaele W.», deine Bubble, deinen Rang und deine Punkte. Wer nicht mitmacht, sieht die Ranglisten gar nicht. Du kannst jederzeit austreten und verschwindest sofort daraus.
  • Das Aussehen deiner Bubble (Farben, Verlauf oder Metall) ist überall sichtbar, wo deine Bubble erscheint: für Personen, die dich finden, für deine Freundinnen und Freunde und in den Ranglisten, wenn du mitmachst.
  • Feedback: Ein gemeldetes Problem oder Feedback bringt angemeldet Punkte, das Feedback selbst wird aber ohne Verbindung zu deinem Konto gespeichert.
  • Fehlerberichte: Ein Fehlerbericht, den du angemeldet mit «Report a bug» sendest, bringt 50 Punkte, sobald wir ihn geprüft haben. Bis dahin und 90 Tage nach unserem Entscheid behält dein Konto eine Verknüpfung dazu: den Anfang deiner Nachricht, den Stand (ausstehend, belohnt oder nicht belohnt) und, falls nicht belohnt, unsere kurze Begründung. Das sehen nur du und die Admins von frooms.

Was wir aus der Nutzung von frooms lernen

Wir bewahren beendete Check-ins auf, um zu verstehen, welche Räume und Lernplätze wann und wie lange genutzt werden, und um frooms damit zu verbessern (zum Beispiel, indem wir zeigen, wie voll ein Ort üblicherweise ist).

  • Dafür zählen wir Check-ins nur über alle Nutzerinnen und Nutzer zusammen. Wir werten nicht aus, was eine einzelne Person tut, und erstellen keine Profile.
  • Punkte zählen wir pro Konto nur, um dir deine eigene Street Cred und, wenn du mitmachst, die Ranglisten zu zeigen. Sonst wird nichts daraus abgeleitet, und niemand wird anderweitig danach beurteilt oder eingestuft.
  • Statistiken, die wir anzeigen oder veröffentlichen, lassen nie auf eine Person schliessen.
  • Nach 12 Monaten, und sofort, wenn du dein Konto löschst, entfernen wir die Verbindung zwischen einem Check-in und dir. Übrig bleiben ein Ort und Zeiten, ohne Bezug zu einer Person.
  • Wir verwenden deine Daten nicht für Werbung, verkaufen sie nicht und geben sie niemandem weiter, auch nicht den Hochschulen.

Wir zeichnen nicht auf, wonach du suchst, wessen Profil du ansiehst oder welche Seiten du angemeldet öffnest. Die Check-ins deiner Freundinnen und Freunde werden alle 2 Minuten abgerufen, solange frooms offen ist; auch das wird nicht aufgezeichnet.
